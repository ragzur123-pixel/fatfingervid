# HyperFrames Brief: Stock Order Swap Visual

- **Workflow**: `/motion-graphics`
- **Deliverable**: 15s Motion Graphic Visual for YouTube Documentary
- **Resolution**: 1920x1080 (16:9)
- **Framerate**: 60fps
- **Composition ID**: `stock-order-swap`

## Concept
A dramatic financial terminal visualization depicting a famous trading "fat finger" / swap error. 
Numbers auto-populate into a stock order ticket (Quantity: 610,000 | Price: $1.00), pause under intense visual scrutiny, dynamic swap places (Quantity: 1 | Price: $610,000.00), and trigger a pulsing green "CONFIRM ORDER" action.
